Who Am I?

I've spent the last several years working both sides of competitive gaming's camera, building audiences as a creator, and building composure as a coach. On the content side, that's meant original storytelling and multi-platform strategy that's reached over 25 million views and generated $100,000+ in revenue. On the coaching side, it's meant sitting with RLCS and CRL players through the pressure of live competition, working on mindset and flow state rather than mechanics.

In between, I've led marketing teams, managed broadcasts from the OBS chair, and mentored the people around me, as a Resident Assistant, in HR, and coaching kids' soccer on weekends. The throughline is the same: helping people and brands perform when it counts.
